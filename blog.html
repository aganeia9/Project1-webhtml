How I chose art as my hobby

I started drawing as young as 5 years old. Back then i saw drawing as something fun, just a child playing with their toy. I didn't see art as passion until when there's college students coming to my community. i get to know them as a elementary student and one of the college student is an artist. Her art captivated me, it makes me want to make art as good as her so that's when art became as a passion. Art isn't just about fun or something to pass time, from that point i see art as something to express myself and give me joy. Time goes on and i eventually choose digital art as my main art media.

I begin making digital art from my phone using just my fingers. Yes, it is hard at first but i get the hang of it pretty fast, my fingers became my pen. Few of the first digital art i made with my fingers satisfy me enough back then, each drawing show improvement from the last.



My first digital art

What you are seeing is my first digital art i made in 2022 that i consider as a complete work, also the first finished art i made with my Ipad. I'm not proud of it, but it shows how much i improved. My skill grew gradually from that and i switch software, from Ibispaint to Pocreate. And from that change of tools my art improved a lot that's why i think if you feel like your skill stop showing improvement you should try getting new tools.



Finding new Artstyle

Back then my artstyle is not consisten at all so i try to find a new one that i think i can draw it consistenly, i really haven't find one to this day. And so i decided to try realism, that's the solution i got when i browse the internet. The people that went to art school are pushed to learn realism first before finding their own unique artstyle, for me it make sense.

This is my first attempt on realism, i just try to recreate already existing image.

Then i try to learn more fundamentals and i fixated on colors. I've been observing other artist's drawing and what i get is color always be the one thing that make art nice to look at.
